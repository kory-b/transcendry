# Transcendry Business Summary & Website Planning Brief

## 1. Business Name

**Company name:** Transcendry LLC
**Public brand:** Transcendry
**Preferred domain:** transcendry.com
**Business type:** Side consulting/advisory business, initially operated by a single founder while maintaining a full-time job.

The name **Transcendry** was selected after exploring several naming territories including pathfinding, S-curve/growth, pinnacle, and transformation. The final direction centers on transformation, practical elevation, and helping companies move beyond their current operating constraints.

The name suggests:

- Transcending current limitations
- Practical transformation
- Digital/data/AI maturity
- Building the next version of how work gets done
- A modern but professional consulting identity

Avoid over-indexing the brand around “loop,” “S-curve,” or “pinnacle.” Those were explored but did not feel right as the core brand.

## 2. Founder Context

The founder is a senior digital/data/product leader with deep hands-on and strategic experience across:

- Digital transformation
- Data platforms
- Azure

- SQL/Python
- SAP data
- Power BI
- Analytics applications
- AI-enabled workflows
- Agentic workflow design
- Product management
- Agile delivery

The founder currently has a full-time job and family responsibilities, so the business must start as a constrained, low-risk side consulting operation. The business should not require daily client support, production firefighting, or a large time commitment.

The goal is not to immediately replace full-time income. The initial goal is to prove that an outside client will pay meaningfully for the founder’s expertise.

## 8. Services / Offers

Initial offers should be fixed-scope, advisory-heavy, and designed to fit around the founder’s full-time job and family constraints.

### Offer 1: AI/Data Opportunity Assessment

A short advisory engagement to identify where AI, automation, analytics, or data products could create real business value.

Deliverables may include:

- Current-state workflow assessment
- Top automation/data/AI opportunities
- Value vs. feasibility ranking
- Risk and complexity rating
- Recommended next steps
- Build/buy/avoid guidance
- 30–90 day roadmap

Suggested first-client price range:

- $1,500–$3,500 initially
- Later $5,000–$10,000+ as credibility and repeatability increase

---

### Offer 2: Manual Workflow to Digital Roadmap

A fixed-scope sprint focused on one manual or messy business workflow.

Deliverables may include:

- Workflow map
- Pain point diagnosis
- Data/tooling assessment
- Automation opportunities
- Target-state process
- Recommended architecture
- MVP roadmap

---

### Offer 3: Prototype Sprint

A small proof-of-concept engagement, not a production implementation.

Possible prototype examples:

- Lightweight internal analytics app
- Power BI reporting improvement
- Databricks/SQL data model prototype
- AI-assisted workflow concept
- Decision-support tool mockup
- Automation proof of concept

Important boundary:
Prototype work should not include open-ended production support unless separately scoped.

---

### Offer 4: Fractional Advisory

A limited retainer model once trust exists.

Possible structure:

- 2 calls per month
- Async review of strategy, architecture, roadmap, or backlog
- Guidance for internal teams
- No emergency support
- No daily availability

## 9. First Business Goal

The first goal is not scale.

The first goal is:

**One client, one clean engagement, one check, zero chaos.**

The business should be designed around proof and optionality, not pressure.

Initial success criteria:

- A real outside client pays for the founder’s expertise
- The work is deliverable in 2–5 hours per week
- The engagement does not conflict with the founder’s full-time role
- The client sees value
- The model can be repeated

- Fixed-scope engagements
- Strategy/advisory first
- Build/prototype later

## 12. LLC / Legal Setup Summary

The business is being formed as an Ohio LLC.

Recommended legal name:

**Transcendry LLC**

Filing path:

- Use the official Ohio Secretary of State business filing portal
- Submit a Business Filing, not a UCC filing
- File Articles of Organization for a Domestic Limited Liability Company

Purpose clause recommendation:

“The purpose of the limited liability company is to engage in any lawful act or activity for which a limited liability company may be formed under the laws of the State of Ohio.”

Alternative more descriptive purpose clause:

“The purpose of the limited liability company is to provide consulting, advisory, technology, data, artificial intelligence, digital transformation, and related business services, and to engage in any lawful act or activity for which a limited liability company may be formed under the laws of the State of Ohio.”

Recommendation:
Use the broad lawful-purpose clause unless the filing portal forces more specificity.

## 13. Statutory Agent

The founder can serve as the Ohio statutory agent if:

- The founder is an Ohio resident
- The founder has a physical Ohio street address
- The founder is comfortable with that address being public
- The founder can reliably receive legal/government mail

However, for privacy, a commercial statutory agent service may be preferable.

Recommendation:
Use a registered/statutory agent service if privacy matters. Use the founder only if simplicity and cost matter more than privacy.

## 15. Payment Logistics

The minimum viable payment stack:

1. Form Ohio LLC
2. Get EIN from IRS
3. Open business checking account
4. Create W-9
5. Create simple contract/SOW template
6. Set up invoicing
7. Accept ACH/check payments
8. Track income and expenses
9. Set aside taxes
10. Consult CPA for first-year setup

Preferred client payment flow:

Client says yes
→ Send SOW/contract
→ Client signs
→ Send W-9 if requested
→ Invoice upfront payment
→ Client pays by ACH/check
→ Money lands in business checking
→ Move 30–40% to tax savings
→ Do the work
→ Invoice final payment
→ Record income and expenses

Recommended payment terms:

- 50% upfront / 50% before final deliverable, or
- 100% upfront for small advisory engagements under roughly $3,000

Avoid:

- Waiting until all work is complete to collect payment
- Personal Venmo/Cash App
- Mixing personal and business funds
- Net 30 terms for small early clients if avoidable

## 16. Taxes / Accounting Notes

The founder should likely operate initially as a single-member LLC taxed as a disregarded entity unless a CPA recommends otherwise.

Do not elect S-Corp status on day one unless income justifies the added complexity.

Set aside a conservative 30–40% of consulting revenue for taxes until a CPA gives a more precise number.

Track:

- Revenue
- Software subscriptions
- Legal/accounting costs
- Business banking fees
- Payment processing fees
- Domain/hosting/email costs
- Professional insurance
- Business mileage, if any
- Equipment used for the business, if applicable

A CPA should be consulted on:

- Estimated taxes
- Ohio tax treatment
- Whether any services are taxable
- Deductibility of expenses
- Whether/when S-Corp election makes sense
